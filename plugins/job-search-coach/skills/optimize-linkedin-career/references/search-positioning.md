# Search positioning

Separate profile-language recommendations from claims about the present market. A current title, keyword, employer, compensation, or demand recommendation needs a dated, still-current vacancy from an official employer page or LinkedIn Jobs. Cite its date, source, geography, and eligibility constraint. Expired listings do not establish current demand.

Without that evidence, label keywords and target titles `hypothesis` and route the request to `research-target-job-market`; do not imply recruiter ranking or outcome. Check location, remote geography, work authorization, employment/contract eligibility, time zone, language, and compensation currency before calling a role viable. US-remote is not evidence of US eligibility.

Write headlines and About sections as drafts. Retain `candidate-reported` labels for source facts and `[confirm: ...]` placeholders for unsupported details.

## Target-technology decision routes

For a user-requested target technology such as Jenkins, produce three routes in `rewrites`: `if_jenkins_confirmed`, `if_jenkins_unconfirmed`, and `if_no_jenkins_experience`. Replace `jenkins` with the requested technology name when different. The confirmed route may use the technology only with supported scope. The unconfirmed route must keep it out of headline and About claims while asking for proof. The no-experience route should reposition adjacent evidence truthfully, such as CI/CD automation, GitLab CI, Python, Bash, Kubernetes, release support, or platform reliability, without implying direct experience.

Add `decision_rationale` wherever a keyword is confirmed, omitted, or held for proof. Use current vacancy evidence only for market claims. Without that evidence, the decision can still guide profile language as a hypothesis, but it must not claim current demand, recruiter ranking, or hiring outcomes.

For target-technology requests, the `linkedin_edit_packet` must make the headline decision explicit. If Jenkins or another requested technology lacks supported evidence, keep the copy-ready headline and About free of that claim, set the affected rows to `confirm_or_omit=omit` or `confirm`, and name the safe adjacent positioning. If evidence is later confirmed, use scoped language only, such as pipeline automation, agents, shared libraries, migration, or release support, tied to the confirmed fact ID.

When target role lanes are proposed, add one `linkedin_target_vacancy_alignment_card=profile_to_current_vacancy_fit_gate` row before final keyword decisions. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `source_positioning_board_id=role_lane_decision_map`, `target_vacancy_state=supplied_current|supplied_stale|not_supplied`, `vacancy_source_required=dated_current_official_employer_or_linkedin_jobs_source`, `current_vacancy_source`, `candidate_fact_match_state=full|partial_profile_fit_not_vacancy_fit|blocked|unknown`, `must_have_gap_check`, `nice_to_have_gap_check`, `keyword_decision_rule=use_profile_language_as_hypothesis_until_vacancy_and_candidate_fact_both_support_use`, `profile_copy_decision`, `research_handoff=research-target-job-market`, `candidate_questions`, `blocked_claims`, `next_safe_action=collect_target_vacancy_or_keep_keywords_as_hypotheses`, `no_external_action=true`, and `draft_only=true`. The row decides whether the profile language is supported by a current vacancy and candidate facts, or whether it remains a hypothesis. It must separate must-have constraints such as eligibility, authorization, location, work mode, compensation process, required tools, production scope, and role level from nice-to-have gaps such as optional tools, certifications, public proof, or preferred experience. Do not call the candidate a fit, Top Applicant, high-demand match, salary match, or interview-ready for a vacancy without both dated vacancy evidence and supported candidate facts.

## Keyword evidence matrix

Return `keyword_evidence_matrix` rows with: phrase/title variant, dated vacancy source, geography/arrangement, candidate fact ID or `unknown`, safe profile section, and decision `use|confirm|omit`. A `use` decision requires a dated current vacancy source and supported candidate fact; use `confirm` when a candidate fact is `unknown:` or needs confirmation, and `omit` when support is absent. Preserve the canonical prefix on every material claim. Do not infer current market demand from a static keyword list.
