# Interview evaluation rubrics

Use a weighted scorecard, never an unsupported overall impression or numeric readiness score. Score only an observed candidate response. Before a response, every score and weighted total is `unknown:`; do not infer performance or predict hiring.

## Core rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Observable evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Requirement relevance | 30 | Answer addresses its vacancy requirement ID. |
| Fact grounding | 25 | Claims cite a candidate fact ID and stay within it. |
| Reasoning and tradeoffs | 20 | Assumptions, decisions, and limits are explicit. |
| Communication | 15 | Structured, concise response that answers the question. |
| Questions and judgment | 10 | Candidate identifies a relevant unknown or decision point. |

Weights total 100. A weighted scorecard records criterion, weight, score, observed evidence, and weighted contribution. Do not give feedback such as “be confident” without a question ID and observable criterion. For technical deep dive or system design, redistribute only the 10 available points from Questions and judgment to vacancy-specific technical reasoning; document the source criterion, receiving criterion, point amount, and resulting weights, which must remain within the available points and total 100. For a recruiter screen or offer-stage, technical criterion is `not applicable` unless supported by the vacancy and stage.

## Score anchors and stage-aware weighting

Use these score anchors only for observed, fact-bounded responses: `0` = no responsive evidence; `1` = materially incomplete, unsupported, or unclear evidence; `2` = partly responsive evidence with a material gap; `3` = clear, relevant, fact-grounded evidence; `4` = clear, relevant, fact-grounded evidence that also explicitly addresses the stated requirement, constraint, or tradeoff. An unsupported claim is `unknown:`, not a score increase.

For recruiter screen, hiring-manager, behavioral loop, panel, take-home, technical screen, technical deep dive, system design, and offer-stage, retain the core weights unless the requested stage and vacancy support the documented redistribution rule above. Stage-aware weighting notes must identify the stage, source criterion, receiving criterion, point amount, reason, and resulting weights; where technical reasoning is not supported, mark it `not applicable` with the reason rather than inferring it.

## Feedback format

Use: `inferred: question ID=Q-###; criterion=<name>; observation=<what was said>; next practice=<bounded change>`. If the answer includes an unsupported claim, identify it as `unknown:` and request confirmation rather than coaching it as true. A scorecard is for practice, does not predict an interview result, and cannot establish a company process.
