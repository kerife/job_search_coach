Score anchors: `0` = no responsive evidence; `1` = materially incomplete, unsupported, or unclear evidence; `2` = partly responsive evidence with a material gap; `3` = clear, relevant, fact-grounded evidence; `4` = clear, relevant, fact-grounded evidence that explicitly addresses the stated requirement, constraint, or tradeoff. Unsupported claims remain `unknown:` and require candidate clarification.

Stage-aware weighting notes: record `stage=`, `source criterion=`, `receiving criterion=`, `point amount=`, `reason=`, and `resulting weights=`. Apply a redistribution only when the stated stage and vacancy support it. For recruiter screen, hiring-manager, behavioral loop, panel, take-home, technical screen, technical deep dive, system design, or offer-stage, mark unsupported technical weighting `not applicable` with the reason.

Weighted total: `unknown:` until all scored observations are available. Cite the candidate fact ID for each material answer claim. Mark a criterion or stage `not applicable` only with its reason, then normalize remaining weights to 100 and record the adjustment.
