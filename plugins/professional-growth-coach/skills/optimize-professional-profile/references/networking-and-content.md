# Networking and content

Prepare connection notes, recruiter messages, recommendation requests, comments, and posts as drafts only. Personalize from a visible source or candidate-provided context; do not assert a relationship, vacancy, skill, result, or authorization not supplied.

Before recommending Featured material or content, perform an explicit confidentiality review: ownership, employer policy/NDA, customer data, identifiers, internal architecture, screenshots, dashboards, code, projects, and approval. Do not propose sanitized internal architectures/projects or derivatives without that review. Prefer candidate-owned, public, or clearly approved material.

No draft permits sending, connecting, posting, publishing, uploading, applying, or sharing. Immediately before execution, obtain explicit authorization naming the exact action, exact target, and exact final content or asset identity when content or assets apply.

## Outreach funnel

Return `outreach_funnel` rows as a prioritized outreach sequence, not a pile of messages. Required fields: contact category, evidence source, draft type, relationship context, `priority_rank`, `sequence_step`, `cadence_window`, `personalization_trigger`, `success_signal`, `next_safe_action`, `measurement_event`, stop condition, and exact authorization gate. Use the draft types `connection_note`, `recruiter_interest`, `recruiter_conversation_bridge`, `referral_request`, and `follow_up_stop_condition` where applicable. Each material item keeps its canonical evidence prefix.

`priority_rank` and `sequence_step` are 1–5 integers. Put the safest, best-context target first; do not rank generic cold outreach above a named recruiter, warm relationship, or confirmed vacancy context. `cadence_window` describes when the draft would be reviewed or considered, not an automatic send schedule. `personalization_trigger` must be visible or candidate-provided context, never `generic`. `success_signal` is an observable reply, qualification, referral, recruiter-screen booking, or useful process information; do not call it a guaranteed interview. `next_safe_action` must be one of `collect_recipient_context`, `draft_only_review`, `wait_for_candidate_authorization`, or `record_observation_only`. `measurement_event` should map to a candidate-isolated `linkedin_funnel_events` ID where available.

Authorization naming the exact action, exact target, and exact final content or asset identity when content or assets apply is required immediately before any send, connection request, post, publication, upload, application, or share; drafting does not authorize execution.
