# Experiments

Capture a dated baseline: profile views, search appearances, post impressions, relevant recruiter contacts, applications, responses, interviews, offers, and time invested—only if visible or candidate-reported. Mark unavailable values.

Use 14/30/60/90-day check-ins. Define one hypothesis, one reversible profile/content change where practical, date, audience/geography, measurement window, and confounders. Do not attribute changes to LinkedIn algorithm behavior or any single intervention when other variables changed. Report observed results, limitations, and next safe draft.

## Intervention registry

When an audit recommends public profile copy, content, outreach, or proof-asset changes, add one `linkedin_intervention_registry` row in `experiment_plan`. This row turns the recommendation into a measurable, reversible coaching experiment. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `linkedin_intervention_registry=profile_content_asset_change_log`, `profile_version_id`, `source_scorecard_id`, `intervention_type`, `intervention_summary`, `baseline_window`, `observation_window=14_30_60_90_days`, `target_audience`, `baseline_metrics`, `confounders_to_log`, `privacy_boundary=aggregate_candidate_owned_metrics_no_raw_viewer_identity_no_private_profile_text`, `decision_options=continue,pause,revert,research`, `draft_only=true`, and `causality_boundary=observational_signals_not_attribution_or_guarantee`. Metrics should be aggregate and candidate-owned only: profile views, search appearances, profile appearances, post impressions, qualified contacts, conversations, recruiter screens, applications, interviews, offers, and time invested when visible or candidate-reported.

Add one `linkedin_funnel_cohort_snapshot` row for the first review state, even when metrics are still unavailable. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `linkedin_funnel_cohort_snapshot=weekly_aggregate_signal_review`, `profile_version_id`, `snapshot_date`, `metric_window=weekly`, `profile_views`, `search_appearances`, `profile_appearances`, `post_impressions`, `qualified_contacts`, `conversations`, `recruiter_screens`, `quality_signal`, `decision`, `next_action`, `draft_only=true`, `no_external_action=true`, and `causality_boundary=observational_signals_not_attribution_or_guarantee`. `decision` must be one of `continue`, `pause`, `revert`, or `research`; use `research` when no post-change data exists. Never store viewer identities, raw profile text, contact details, private URLs, or individual third-party activity.

Immediately after the cohort snapshot, add one `linkedin_weekly_experiment_decision_card=coach_signal_to_action_review` row. This row turns the measured signals into a professional coaching decision instead of a passive metric list. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `linkedin_weekly_experiment_decision_card=coach_signal_to_action_review`, `source_registry_id`, `source_snapshot_id`, `profile_version_id`, `review_cadence`, `primary_question`, `minimum_observation_window`, `input_metrics`, `quality_bar`, `decision_rules`, `current_decision`, `next_action`, `confounder_check`, `coach_note`, `privacy_boundary=aggregate_candidate_owned_metrics_no_viewer_identity_no_private_profile_text`, `outcome_boundary=not_a_causal_claim_response_rate_interview_salary_or_time_to_hire_prediction`, `draft_only=true`, and `no_external_action=true`. The card must prefer qualified contacts, conversations, recruiter screens, and target-role context over raw views or appearances. `current_decision` must be one of `continue`, `pause`, `revert`, or `research`, and should match the cohort snapshot until new evidence justifies a change. Never claim causal uplift, response rate, interview probability, salary impact, time-to-hire reduction, ranking, or guaranteed outcome.

Immediately after the decision card, add exactly two `linkedin_measurement_review_checkpoint=post_change_signal_review_gate` rows. These are the coach-facing 14/30-day gates that make the experiment operational. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `source_registry_id=profile_content_asset_change_log`, `profile_version_id`, `checkpoint=day_14|day_30`, `calendar_timing`, `metrics_to_compare`, `quality_question`, `decision_threshold`, `confounders_to_review`, `allowed_decisions=continue,pause,revert,research`, `next_action_if_signal_positive`, `next_action_if_signal_weak`, `privacy_boundary=aggregate_candidate_owned_metrics_no_viewer_identity_no_private_profile_text`, `causality_boundary=observation_not_causation`, `outcome_boundary=not_a_response_rate_interview_salary_or_time_to_hire_prediction`, `draft_only=true`, and `no_external_action=true`. Compare profile views, search appearances, qualified contacts, conversations, recruiter screens, and time invested, but do not treat raw views as sufficient. Use day 14 to decide whether to keep observing, pause, or research; use day 30 to decide whether to continue, make one reversible next improvement, revert, or research the weakest assumption. Always log confounders such as profile edits, networking activity, applications, content posts, referrals, seasonality, availability, and market changes.

Immediately after the two checkpoints, add one `linkedin_14_30_signal_readout=coach_baseline_to_decision_review` row. This is the client-facing measurement interpretation that a professional coach would use after the first two observation windows. Required fields are `candidate_id`, `source_registry_id=profile_content_asset_change_log`, `source_decision_card_id=coach_signal_to_action_review`, `profile_version_id`, `baseline_readiness=ready|partial|missing`, `baseline_required_before_change`, `day_14_review`, `day_30_review`, `primary_quality_signal`, `noise_signal`, `decision_framework`, `current_decision`, `confounders_to_check`, `privacy_boundary=aggregate_candidate_owned_metrics_no_viewer_identity_no_raw_profile_text`, `causality_boundary=observed_signals_only_not_single_change_attribution`, `outcome_boundary=not_a_response_rate_interview_salary_time_to_hire_or_ranking_prediction`, `next_safe_action`, `draft_only=true`, and `no_external_action=true`. The readout must require profile views, search appearances, qualified contacts, and conversations as baseline inputs; prioritize qualified contacts or conversations over raw views; name raw views as a noise signal; include continue, pause, revert, and research in the decision framework; check profile edits, networking activity, applications, content posts, and market changes as confounders; and keep the next step private with no publish or message action.

## LinkedIn funnel events

Return `linkedin_funnel_events` as dated candidate-isolated observations for profile view/search appearance, qualified contact, conversation, referral/application, recruiter screen, interview, source, version, and confounders. Preserve the canonical evidence prefix for each material observation and record unavailable values as `unknown:`. These events support observation and uncertainty reporting only; they do not establish recruiter ranking, causal uplift, response rates, interviews, salaries, or time-to-hire.
